Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to book?

No. Tony’s is a walk-in barbers, open 7 days a week. If you’re tight on time, give the shop a call on 07495 646270 before you head over and we’ll let you know how busy it is.

How do I pay?

Cash, card, contactless and mobile pay (Apple Pay and Google Pay) are all accepted. Card payments are handled by Teya.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Kids 12 and under are £12, or £15 for a kids skin fade. We take our time so younger clients are comfortable in the chair.

Do you offer OAP rates?

Yes. Pensioners get a reduced rate of £12 for a cut.

How much is a beard trim?

Beard work varies with length and style, so we price it in the shop. Just ask when you come in.

Where are you?

12 The Parade, Exmouth, Devon EX8 1RW, in the town centre. Tap “Get directions” to open the route in Apple Maps or Google Maps.
