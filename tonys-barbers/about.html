About & reviews

Exmouth’s favourite chair.

A friendly, no-fuss barbers on The Parade, trusted by Exmouth for clean fades, classic cuts and proper hot towel shaves.

The shop

Built on the details.

Walk into Tony’s and you’ll find a clean, modern shop with the hexagon lights overhead, a drink on the house and a barber who takes the time to listen.

Whether it’s a sharp skin fade with a design, a tidy-up before work, a first haircut or a traditional hot towel shave, every client gets the same care. That’s why Exmouth voted us the town’s best barber shop.
