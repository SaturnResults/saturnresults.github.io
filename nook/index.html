What's inside

Built for people who run client work. Everything one hover away.

We built Saturn Nook for ourselves, to stop losing track of hours and to-dos across a dozen client sites. It worked well enough that we're giving it away.

Clients and projects

Every client with a status from lead to live, their website, project folder, hourly rate and notes. Open their site, folder or code in one click.

Billable-hours timer

Start a timer per client. It keeps running beside the notch, adds up the week in pounds, and exports a timesheet ready for invoicing.

A scratchpad for call notes with one-click timestamps, and a row of buttons for the apps and sites you open all day.

Install in a minute

Four steps. One of them is a macOS security check.

  1. Download and unzip

    Click Download for Mac. Your Mac unzips it into Saturn Nook in your Downloads folder.

  2. Move it to Applications

    Drag Saturn Nook into your Applications folder in Finder.

  3. Allow it the first time

    Double-click it. macOS will say it can't check the app for malicious software. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Saturn Nook, then confirm. You only do this once.

  4. Hover over your notch

    That's it. Move your pointer to the top-middle of the screen and the Nook slides open. The ⬡ icon in your menu bar has Launch at Login, so it's there every time you start your Mac.

Why the security step? Apple shows that message for apps that aren't sold through the App Store or registered with Apple's paid developer programme. Saturn Nook is a small free tool, so it isn't yet. It doesn't connect to the internet and never sends your data anywhere.

Questions

Good to know

Does my Mac need a notch?

No. On a Mac without a notch, or on an external monitor, Saturn Nook sits as a small black tab at the top-middle of the screen and opens the same way when you hover over it.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/SaturnNook. There's no account, no cloud sync and no tracking. Back up that folder to keep your clients, tasks and hours.

Why does it ask for Calendar and Spotify access?

Calendar access shows today's meetings on the home tab. Spotify or Music access lets the play, pause and skip buttons work. Both are optional, and you can change them any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Is it really free?

Yes. No trial, no upgrade and no ads. If it's useful, tell a friend who runs their own business.

How do I remove it?

Click the ⬡ icon in the menu bar and choose Quit, then drag Saturn Nook from Applications to the Bin. To remove your data as well, delete the SaturnNook folder in ~/Library/Application Support.
