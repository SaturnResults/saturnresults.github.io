What we check

A customer decides about you long before they meet you.

Most of that happens on a phone, in about forty seconds, and you never see any of it. These are the six places it goes wrong, and the twenty four things we actually look at.

01

Can they find you?

If you do not come up, nothing else on this list matters.

  • Your Google Business Profile, and what is missing from it
  • Whether you rank for what you sell plus near me
  • Whether AI assistants name you when asked for your trade locally
  • Your name, address and phone matching everywhere they appear
02

Do they trust you?

A strong rating from two years ago reads as closed down.

  • Your rating, and how recent the last review actually is
  • Whether you reply to reviews, especially the unhappy ones
  • Real photos of your place, or stock images anyone can spot
  • Whether your social looks alive or abandoned
03

Does your site do its job?

Over half your visitors are on a phone, often on patchy signal.

  • How long it takes to load on mobile data
  • Whether it is clear what you sell within five seconds
  • How it behaves on a small or older phone
  • Whether anything is broken, missing or out of date
04

Can they actually buy?

The most expensive faults are the ones nobody tells you about.

  • Whether your contact form genuinely sends, tested properly
  • Whether your number taps to call on a phone
  • Your opening hours matching across Google, your site and social
  • Whether they can book or pay without having to ring you
05

Is anything scaring them off?

Browsers now warn people away, loudly, and you would never know.

  • Whether your site shows a not secure warning
  • Whether your domain or certificate is close to expiring
  • Whether your emails are landing in spam folders
  • Whether you still email customers from a free address
06

How you compare

The part most people find hardest to read, and most useful.

  • Two nearby rivals who are getting this right
  • What they are doing online that you are not
  • Where they beat you in search, and by how far
  • The one thing that would close the gap fastest
