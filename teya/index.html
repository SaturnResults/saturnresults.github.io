What we look at

Four places customers judge you before they ever walk in.

Your website

How quickly it loads, how it looks on a phone, whether it is clear what you do, and whether someone can actually get in touch without giving up. If you have not got a site, we say whether you genuinely need one.

Your Google listing

Whether you show up when someone nearby searches for what you sell. Opening hours, photos, categories and reviews. This is usually the quickest thing to fix and the one most businesses are leaving money on.

Your social media

Whether your pages look alive or abandoned, whether they match what you actually do now, and whether anything there is putting people off. We will tell you if posting more would not help you.

Your online security

Whether your site has a valid certificate, whether browsers are warning people away from it, whether your domain is about to expire and whether anything is publicly exposed that should not be.
