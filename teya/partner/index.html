What to say

Say it as a thank you, never as an offer

The moment is right after everything is working and they are happy. Do not build it up. The less it sounds like a pitch, the more of them take it.

The line“Right, you are all set. One more thing, and it is free. Because you have signed up with us, you get an online check from the agency we work with. They go through your Google listing, your website and your socials and tell you what is working and what is not. Costs you nothing, takes them a couple of days.”

Then, straight away“Scan this and it is done in two minutes.”

Why it is worded that way

“It is free” lands before they have a chance to wonder what it costs. “The agency we work with” makes it a benefit of joining rather than you selling something on the side. And handing them the QR while you are still talking is what turns a yes into an actual claim.

In person

Yes, you can do it there and then

This is far and away the best way. Someone who says “email it to me” almost never does it. Someone who scans it while you are stood there does.

  1. Wait until the machine is working and they are pleased. Not before. It has to feel like a thank you, not part of the sale.
  2. Say the line above. Thirty seconds, no build up.
  3. Open the show card and hold your phone up so they can scan it with their camera. Their phone, their details, their typing.
  4. If they cannot scan it, ask for their mobile and send them the text message below while you are stood with them.
  5. If they ask you to just fill it in, you can, but read their email back to them before you send it. One wrong letter and the whole thing is wasted.
  6. That is you done. Saturn Results takes it from there and comes back to them within 48 hours.

Do

  • Offer it to every single new signup, no picking and choosing
  • Say free early and say it plainly
  • Get the QR in front of them while you are still there
  • Let them type their own email
  • Say “no catch, it is just part of signing up”

Do not

  • Ask “would you be interested in a free audit?” It invites a no
  • Promise what the check will find, or that it will get them customers
  • Describe it as a sales call or a consultation
  • Sign them up without telling them a third party will contact them
  • Use it as a way to save a signup that is going badly
