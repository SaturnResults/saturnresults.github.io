The problem

Marketplace booking apps hold all the cards.

They can be a reasonable place to start. The trouble tends to arrive later, once your clinic is busy and the terms are no longer yours to set.

01

The fees add up

Marketplace platforms often charge a commission on bookings, a monthly fee, or both. They are rarely cheap, and the cost can grow in step with how well your clinic is doing. The better your month, the bigger the bill.

02

The booking happens on their site

Your patient books on a platform that also lists other clinics. Many of these arrangements mean the relationship, the contact details and the data sit with the platform rather than with you, and you are paying to reach people who were already yours.

03

The alternative has been clunky

Custom and integrated systems have a reputation for looking dated, taking months to build, and costing a great deal to keep running. That reputation is fair, and it is the gap Slate was made to close.

Questions

The things clinics ask us first.

How long does set-up take?

Usually about 48 hours from the point we have what we need from you, which is your services, your appointment lengths and your opening hours. If your setup turns out to be more involved, we will tell you that up front rather than discovering it halfway through.

Who owns the data?

You do. Your patient details, their appointment history and your diary belong to your clinic. You can export them whenever you want, and we do not market to your patients or pass their details to anyone else.

Will it work with the diary or software I already use?

In most cases yes, and we will confirm it properly on a short call before you commit to anything. If it turns out we cannot make it work with what you have, we will tell you honestly rather than sell you something that will not fit.

What happens with cancellations?

Your cancellation policy and notice period are built into the booking, so a patient sees your terms before they confirm. If someone cancels within your notice period the slot reopens, so it can be filled rather than sitting empty.

What support do I get?

You can reach a real person. Changes to your services, prices or hours are part of the monthly fee rather than billed as extras, and we will make them for you if you would rather not.
