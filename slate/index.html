The difference

A marketplace rents you your own clients.

Booking marketplaces are genuinely good at sending you a first appointment. The trouble starts afterwards, because the client, the card fee and the rebooking all stay on their platform rather than yours.

The awkward questions

What everybody asks on the first call.

What happens to next week's diary while we switch?

Nothing. We import your future appointments and run both systems side by side for two weeks, so your old booking page keeps working while Slate fills up behind it. You pick the day you turn the old one off, and we are on the phone that morning.

Am I tied in?

No. Slate is rolling monthly from the first day, with no minimum term. Cancel whenever you like with 30 days' notice and your client list is exported to you in full on the way out. We would rather you stayed because it works than because you were stuck.

Who owns the client data?

You do. We are the processor, you are the controller, and it is written down in a data agreement you sign before we touch anything. You can export the whole list yourself at any point without asking us.

I am tied into a contract with my card machine provider.

Then we pay it off. Slate runs card payments through Teya, and Teya covers what is left of your existing contract so you can move now instead of waiting out your term. Tell us who you are with and how long is left, and we will confirm the payout in writing before you commit to anything.

Will I lose the reviews I have built up?

Reviews on a third party marketplace stay there, and that is true of any move. What we do is point Slate at your Google listing from day one, because Google reviews are the ones new clients actually read before they book.

My team are not technical.

That is most of the clinics we work with. We set it up for you rather than handing over a login and wishing you luck, we train the team in a session before go live, and there is a person at the end of the phone afterwards.

How quickly can we be taking bookings?

Within a week in most cases. We build and brand it, import your clients, services and future appointments, then train your team before you go live. The two weeks of parallel running happen after that, so nothing is rushed on your side.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

No. Slate gives us an embed code that drops into a page on your existing site, so it works with anything that lets you paste in a snippet, which is almost everything: WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, Shopify or a hand built site. If your site makes that awkward we will tell you on the first call rather than after you have paid.

Can we take payment at the front desk?

Yes. Deposits and online payments are built in, and for card payments in the clinic there is a Teya terminal that runs on the same rates we quote you. The terminal is priced separately from your monthly fee, and we will put the cost in writing with your rate check.

Do you build the website too?

We do, and Slate is built into it properly when we do. If you already have a site you are happy with, that is fine, Slate goes into that one instead.
