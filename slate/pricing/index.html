Taking payment

Card payments run through Teya, at a rate we will beat.

Your monthly Slate fee and what it costs you to take a card are two separate things. Nobody takes a percentage of your treatment price. The only percentage anywhere near your money is the card fee, and that one is worth getting right.

Why nobody can quote you one honest number

Card processing is not a single rate, and any provider who gives you one before seeing your statement is quoting you an average and hoping. What you actually pay moves with four things:

  • 1The cards your patients use. A UK consumer debit card and a foreign commercial credit card cost very different amounts to accept.
  • 2How they pay. Tapped in the clinic, keyed in over the phone and paid online in advance are three different prices.
  • 3How much you take. Volume moves the rate, which is why a busy clinic should not be on a single-practitioner rate.
  • 4Your average transaction. A fixed pence-per-transaction charge matters far more on a £30 follow-up than on a £90 initial assessment.

Whatever you are paying now, we will beat it.

Send us a recent card statement and we will come back with a Teya rate that is lower than the one you are on. If we cannot beat it, we will tell you that instead of talking you into switching for nothing.

Questions about the money

The things clinics ask before they sign anything.

Does the price go up as I take on more practitioners?

Not on Plug-in or Complete. Those are per clinic, so you can add people without the bill moving. Solo is the exception: it is priced for one practitioner, and when you take on a second you move up to Plug-in or Complete depending on whether you are keeping your own practice management system.

Does Slate replace Cliniko?

No, and on the Plug-in plan that is the whole point. Your Cliniko diary stays the system of record, nobody has to learn anything new and no records move anywhere. Slate is the booking journey that sits on your own website in front of it. If you would rather not pay for a practice management system at all, Complete gives you the dashboard instead.

What if I am on Cliniko now but want to come off it later?

You move from Plug-in to Complete and the dashboard takes over, which is a ten pound a month difference rather than a rebuild. Your bookings and client history come across. Doing it the other way round works too if you decide you want a full practice management system later.

Is there any commission on bookings?

No, on any plan. There is no percentage of your treatment price, no fee per booking and nothing that grows because you had a busy month. The monthly figure on this page is the monthly figure. Card processing is a separate cost and is covered above.

What will card payments actually cost me?

That depends on your card mix, your volume and your average transaction, which is why we ask to see a statement rather than quote you a number we would have to walk back. Whatever rate you are on today, we will come back with a Teya rate that beats it, or tell you plainly that we cannot.

Is there a set-up fee or a minimum term?

We will put the exact figure for your clinic in writing on the call, before you commit to anything, and that includes anything one-off and anything about notice. We would rather you saw the whole number up front than found a line you were not expecting on the first invoice.
