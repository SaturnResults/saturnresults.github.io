How I think about it

Nobody can tell you what to fix before they have looked.

Most agencies lead with whatever they happen to sell. If they build websites, you need a website. If they run ads, you need ads. The diagnosis arrives before anyone has looked at the business, which is the wrong way round and it is why so many owners have paid for something that did nothing.

The first thing a business needs is a proper audit and review, to actually find out what that business needs to grow. Once the core foundations are working and in order, and we have reviewed the ads, then we give a tailored plan for that business.

Harry Bingham
FIRST

Look properly

Your Google listing, your site, your socials, your reviews, what you are already spending and what is actually coming back from it. By hand, not a score out of a hundred from a tool.

THEN

Fix the foundations

There is no point paying for traffic to a site that does not convert, or a listing that does not show up. Foundations first is unglamorous and it is the part that saves people the most money.

AFTER THAT

Review the ads

Only once the basics hold does paid spend make sense. If you are already running ads we go through the account and show you where the money is going.

FINALLY

A plan built for your business

Not a package off a price list. What to do, in what order, with what it should return. If the honest answer is that you do not need us yet, that is what you will get.

If we speak

I will not pitch you on the first call.

I spend it learning about the business. What you sell, who buys it, what a customer is worth, what you have already tried and what went wrong with it. There is no deck and nothing to sit through.

Then I go away and do the audit properly. I come back with what I found, my honest opinion on where the business goes next, and a plan. You get that whether you hire us or not.

Sometimes that plan says spend money with us. Sometimes it says sort your Google listing out first and come back in three months. I would rather tell you that than take a fee for work that was never going to move the needle.

Before this

I did not learn any of this in a lecture theatre.

Saturn Results started in 2023. Before it, and alongside it, there were early starts and jobs that nobody takes for fun. I would not swap them, because between them they taught me more about customers than any course would have.

Door to door sales

Anglian Home Improvements

Four months working a patch to daily targets, generating every conversation from cold. It teaches you how to open with a stranger, how to take a no without it landing, and how fast somebody decides whether they trust you.

Hospitality

Busy bars and a cellar

Cocktail service and bottomless brunches, then running a cellar, signing off deliveries and training new staff. You learn to read a room, work under pressure and that service is the whole product.

Delivery driving

Amazon routes

Planning a route, managing your own day, hitting a number with nobody watching. Unglamorous, and the reason deadlines here are treated as deadlines.

Those jobs are also why I understand the people we work for. Most of our clients are running something hands on, doing the work and the marketing and the books. I know what a long shift feels like, and I know that an agency which wastes your time is worse than no agency at all.
