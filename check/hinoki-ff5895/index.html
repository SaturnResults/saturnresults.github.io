What the name means

The tree
Hinoki is the Japanese cypress, Chamaecyparis obtusa. It is slow-growing, tall and long-lived.
Sacred timber
Since the 7th century it has been the main wood for Japan's great shrines and temples, including Ise Jingū and Hōryū-ji.
Baths and scent
It lines hinoki baths in inns and onsen. The wood is smooth, pale yellow to light amber, with a clean, citrus-like smell.
The word
Hinoki reads hi-no-ki. Some sources translate it as "tree of the sun".
