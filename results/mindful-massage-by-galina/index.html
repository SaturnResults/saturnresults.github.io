Why it's different here

Massage isn't a luxury. It's maintenance.

I'm Galina. I work on my own from one room on Chiswick High Road, which means the hour you book is an hour with me — not with whoever the rota gave you.

Every treatment starts with a complimentary consultation, unrushed, before the clock starts. That's how the massage ends up being yours rather than a routine, and it's why I ask you to come ten or fifteen minutes early.

I keep a wide selection of premium organic and natural essential oils. You choose the blend when you arrive — or we make one up on the spot to suit how you're feeling that day.
