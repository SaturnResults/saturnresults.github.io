About

A space for complete relaxation and renewal.

Galina Gospodinova, massage therapist. One room on Chiswick High Road, and one person in it.

In her words

Massage is more than a luxury.

It is a vital part of self-care, and it can change how your body and your mind feel. That belief is the whole reason this room exists.

I love this work, and what I am really after is simple: that you unwind, that your balance comes back, and that you leave feeling lighter than when you arrived.

Because I work alone, nothing about your appointment is handed off. The person who talks to you before the treatment is the person who gives it, and the person you text afterwards if something still isn't right.

The oils

You choose the blend.

I keep a wide selection of premium organic and natural essential oils. When you arrive you pick the one you want — or we make something up on the spot to suit your mood and what your body needs that day.

It takes a minute and it changes the treatment. Most people who come back start choosing a different blend depending on the week they have had.

The consultation

Come ten minutes early. It's free, and it's the point.

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your appointment. We sit down, unrushed, and talk about what is actually bothering you — where the pain is, what you do all day, how much pressure you want, whether anything has changed since last time.

That conversation is what turns a treatment into your treatment rather than a routine. It is complimentary, and it happens before the clock starts, so an eighty-minute massage is still eighty minutes of hands-on work.
