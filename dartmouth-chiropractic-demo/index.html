About the clinic

Your local clinic, for over a decade.

Martin Robson opened Dartmouth Chiropractic in early 2013, after years in practice in Wales and Cornwall. What began as a chiropractic clinic has since grown into a multidisciplinary health and beauty clinic, with a close team of practitioners many local families have come to know well.

We're still in the same place on Dartmouth Business Park in Townstal, with free parking outside and step-free access for anyone with mobility difficulties. We see patients from Dartmouth, Kingswear, Stoke Fleming, Blackawton and the villages around.

Unhurried appointments, honest advice and care that treats you as a person, not a problem.

Your first visit

Simple, from the first call.

New to the clinic? No jargon and no pressure. Here's what to expect when you come and see us for the first time.

Get in touch

Call or email and we'll book you in with the right practitioner at a time that suits you.

Consultation

We'll talk through your history, lifestyle and symptoms, then carry out a thorough examination.

Treatment

We explain what we've found and agree a plan with you, with treatment usually starting the same day.

Stay well

Leave with simple exercises and advice to keep you moving well between appointments.
